Crush the tablet

Not registered in AU

Open the capsule

Check label

Food

Either — food does not matter

Mixing
CMI gives no instruction on mixing the dose into food or drink; shake the bottle well and measure with an oral syringe or medicine cup, and may be given with food if stomach upset occurs.
Storage
Store the powder and the reconstituted suspension below 25 C (RCH keeps suspensions in the fridge) and discard the reconstituted suspension 14 days after it is made up.
Practical tips
  • Usually three times a day, spaced evenly about every 8 hours
  • Shake the bottle well before measuring; make sure the whole dose is swallowed
  • Capsules: swallow whole with a glass of water (opening not covered by the CMI)
  • Rare superficial tooth discolouration is usually removed by brushing

Before you use this

Scope, sources and limitations

Not a prescribing authority. This tool is clinical decision support for qualified health professionals. It does not replace the full guideline text, a local formulary, or specialist advice. Confirm every dose against the source document and the product label before prescribing or administering.

Neonates are excluded. Regimens for infants under one month of age are age- and gestation-dependent and are deliberately not calculated here. Use ANMF or NeoMedQ neonatal monographs.

Renal and hepatic function. All doses assume normal renal and hepatic function. Adjustments are not modelled.

Dosing source. Regimens are transcribed from CHQ Paediatric Antibiocard: Empirical Antibiotic Guidelines (CHQ-GDL-01202, v10.2), published by Children's Health Queensland.

Formulation strengths used for volume conversion are PBS-listed medicinal product packs (pbs.gov.au).

AMH and eMIMS are not included. Both are licensed subscription products and their monograph text cannot be reproduced here. The dosing layer is designed to be swapped: replace the regimen records with your licensed AMH or eMIMS content and the calculator, caps and volume conversion continue to work unchanged.
