## Findings by topic 1-3

**1. Onboarding: workers, pharmacies and readiness gates**

- **Locumate worker order:** choose role and permanent/shift preference → verify email/mobile → complete profile/documents → await verification → browse opportunities. Listed fields include AHPRA number/expiry, ABN, banking/super, ID, insurance, CV, software, certificates, availability, rates and vaccination capability; which fields apply to each non-pharmacist role is **unverified**. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-sign-up-as-a-job-seeker-shift-work-inc-ft-pt)
- **Locumate pharmacy order:** verify email/mobile → ABN → two store contacts → business/billing addresses → logo → weekday/weekend script volumes → dispensing software/services → team review. Documentation states activation within 24 hours, after which pharmacies can create listings/shifts; the underlying business-verification method is **unverified**. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-create-a-pharmacy-account-on-locumate)
- **Locumate financial collection:** explains bank, ABN, super and TFN collection as supporting payment; AHPRA, ID and insurance support compliance. Collection is documented, but independent evidence of its precise AHPRA-check mechanism is **unverified**. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/why-do-i-need-to-provide-so-many-details-and-documents)
- **LocumCo:** initial CV submission requires contact information, role interest, CV and two references; requests AHPRA number where applicable. Public job browsing exists; exact approval gates before applications, bookings and payouts are **unverified**. [LocumCo](https://locumco.com.au/submit-cv/)
- **Mable worker gate:** approval before offering services/applying requires ABN, NDIS screening incorporating ID verification, infection-control training, two references, photo and bio. Additional checks depend on services. Published verification timing is usually five business days, allowing up to three weeks—not guaranteed time to first work. [Mable](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/will-account-approved/)
- **Mable client/payment gate:** guided signup collects location, support needs, funding, timing and mobile verification. Payment details must be configured before booking; funding may require verification. Its optional sensitive client profile is shared only after a signed agreement. [Mable](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-do-i-sign-up-as-a-client-on-mable/)
- **Mable insurance/first shift:** a signed service agreement must precede the first session; otherwise workers cannot submit support hours for payment and lack the described booking cover. Sessions include personal accident, medical malpractice and liability insurance. It recommends five or more initial applications, rather than promising immediate work. [Mable](https://mable.com.au/discover/approved-support-worker-guide/get-started-support-worker-mable/)
- **Hireup:** approval requires first aid, orientation, NDIS screening, Australian work rights and 100 points of ID; extra checks depend on transport/children/state. Documents go through Cited, with published document verification of 24–72 hours. Its employment model covers tax, super and insurance without requiring workers to operate an ABN business. [Hireup checks](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360016020691-What-do-I-need-to-register-as-a-Hireup-support-worker), [Hireup employment model](https://hireup.com.au/become-a-support-worker/)
- **Sidekicker:** application → industry assessment → work-rights check/profile/OH&S induction → eligibility to apply for matching jobs. Skills categories control visible work. Police checks and drug testing are explicitly not universal standard checks; customer-specific requirements can be arranged. [Sidekicker](https://help.sidekicker.com/en/articles/3843898-understanding-how-sidekicker-screens-and-onboards-workers)
- **Sidekicker hirer gate:** businesses must add a credit card before hiring their first worker; direct debit is an alternative. Charges occur after job completion. This separates account creation from payment readiness. [Sidekicker](https://help.sidekicker.com/en/articles/8320035-how-to-add-a-credit-card)
- **Airtasker payout gate:** its Australia/New Zealand guidance states future earnings are withheld until required account verification is complete. This establishes a payout gate; the same page does not establish that verification universally precedes browsing or offers. [Airtasker](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/42073609183641-Having-problems-verifying-my-account-what-should-I-do)
- **ShiftKey:** professionals submit state licences/credentials, background checks, tax ID/W-9 and payment preference; completed verification enables bidding. Credentials are reusable across facilities and renewal notifications are provided. Legal-name consistency and complete, readable document images are explicitly emphasised. [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey)
- **Clipboard:** first-shift guidance requires government ID, banking information and a background check; role-specific credentials, TB tests or physicals may apply. Facilities can impose certifications, experience requirements and pre-arrival quizzes. Exact pre-verification browsing access is **unverified**. [Clipboard workers](https://www.clipboard.com/for-workers), [Clipboard facilities](https://www.clipboard.com/resources/faq)
- **Nursa:** onboarding covers legal-name/licence information; Wallet setup includes banking, digital W-9 and 1099 delivery preferences. Facilities can access verified credentials; automated screening includes federal and applicable state checks, with some screenings repeated on shift requests. [Nursa onboarding](https://community.nursa.com/help-center/clinician/get-started/), [Nursa screening](https://nursa.com/facility/capabilities/background-checks)
- **CareRev has a clear staged gate:** signup and clinical screening unlock browsing matching shifts; claiming requires the shift’s credentials and facility orientation. Standard checks include professional/state licence, government ID, background check and drug screen, with requirements varying by facility, region and specialty. [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/professionals/faq)
- **IntelyCare:** application plus required credentials → activation → any local training → accepting shifts. Vaccination requirements can be facility-specific and restrict those facilities’ shifts, rather than excluding someone from the whole platform. Employer-provided benefits include workers’ compensation and professional liability insurance. [IntelyCare requirements](https://www.intelycare.com/for-nursing-professionals/nursing/faqs/), [IntelyCare insurance](https://www.intelycare.com/blog/nursing-professionals/get-to-know-your-intelycare-employee-benefits-program/)
- **Incredible Health:** permanent candidates create profiles and receive matches; employers receive pre-vetted candidates matched to role requirements. Employer onboarding includes dedicated support. A uniform marketplace KYC, tax, vaccination or payment gate is **unverified** and should not be inferred from its permanent-hiring model. [Incredible Health](https://www.incrediblehealth.com/employers/)
- **Instawork:** role vetting can include interviews, references, certificates, skills demonstrations/quizzes and attire checks. W-2 access adds ordered identity/profile steps and I-9 work-authorisation verification, including an authorised representative’s in-person document review. [Instawork vetting](https://help.instawork.com/aramark/en/articles/2062195-how-are-instawork-pros-vetted), [Instawork employment onboarding](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/3300620-w-2-faq-how-to-complete-paperwork-and-start-booking-us-based-pros)
- **Wonolo:** in the locations covered by its background-check page, accepting the first job requiring screening triggers the check; clearance is necessary before working. Grow2 W-2 onboarding separately collects W-4, I-9 and direct-deposit information. Geographic exclusions on the background-check page matter. [Wonolo screening](https://info.wonolo.com/background-checks/), [Grow2 onboarding](https://support-grow2.wonolo.com/en_us/how-can-i-sign-up-for-a-w2-opportunity-via-wonolo-%28w-2%29-H1gHWVoalx)
- **Upwork:** identity checks can be triggered by actions such as submitting proposals; ID verification is distinct from skills validation. US freelancers submit W-9 information, while other freelancers use W-8 forms; missing valid tax information can cause withholding. [Upwork identity](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001176427-How-to-verify-your-identity-as-a-freelancer), [Upwork tax](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063948-How-to-complete-Form-W-9-on-Upwork)
- **ShiftRx, dated evidence:** its March 2025 terms require active state-appropriate pharmacy licences and credentialing, and reference assigned onboarding modules. Current execution, gate order and operating status are **unverified**; treat these as documented historical practices. [ShiftRx](https://www.shiftrx.io/terms-march-22-2025)
- **Relief Buddy:** profile photos are checked against government ID. Its terms describe efforts to validate professionals and provider businesses/locations, while explicitly retaining both parties’ due-diligence responsibilities; “pre-vetted” should not be read as unlimited verification assurance. [Relief Buddy identity](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/how-do-i-change-my-profile-picture), [Relief Buddy terms](https://reliefbuddy.com/terms-of-service/)
- **Staffy:** profile/documents → compliance approval → view/apply → facility selection → approved worked hours/payment eligibility. Typical healthcare documents include licence, vulnerable-sector check, immunisation/TB records, CPR/BLS, ID/work eligibility, N95 certificate, résumé and three references. Published onboarding is 2–5 business days after complete uploads. [Staffy](https://staffy.com/faq)
- **Locate a Locum:** booking readiness requires role-specific documentation; pharmacist checklist includes GPhC number, photo ID, enhanced police check and indemnity insurance. Technician/dispenser documentation differs. Exact pre-verification browsing access is **unverified**. [Locate a Locum](https://locatealocum.com/document-checklist)
- **Florence:** app download → application with visible completion progress → interview/mandatory training → review → shifts. Checks cover work history, two employment references, identity, work rights, background screening and professional registration where applicable. [Florence registration](https://www.florence.co.uk/looking-for-work/registration), [Florence review process](https://help.florence.co.uk/en/articles/16002521-what-is-happening-with-my-florence-application)
- **Patchwork:** account → apply to an organisation’s bank → organisation checks/activates → book shifts. Its reusable digital passport avoids repeated uploads, but the organisation owns approval and employment terms. Its employer implementation also includes training, onboarding and migration support. [Patchwork worker approval](https://faq.patchwork.health/i-currently-do-not-work-at-this-organisation-but-want-to-book-bank-shifts.-how-do-i-proceed), [Patchwork implementation](https://patchwork.health/temporary-staffing)
- **Evidence limits:** universal NPI requirements, pharmacy-side KYC/payment gates outside the examples above, and mandatory parking/staffing fields are **unverified**. Locumate verifies the strongest pharmacy-specific setup evidence found: script volume, dispensing software, services and contacts. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-create-a-pharmacy-account-on-locumate)

**2. Talent board / worker discovery**

- **LocumCo offers a genuine reverse job board:** employers can browse pharmacist profiles and proactively connect, alongside ordinary job applications. Detailed search filters, contact-field masking and access restrictions are **unverified**. [LocumCo](https://locumco.com.au/locumco-candidate/)
- **Locumate supports relationship-based discovery:** internal pools, directly assigned internal shifts, preferred locums considered first, and escalation to agencies when internal/preferred workers are unavailable. A broad searchable public worker directory is **unverified**. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/locumate-december-2024-release-notes)
- **Mable profiles expose decision-useful information:** qualifications, reviews, interests, languages and indicative rates; its product advertises 70+ filters. Full-name searching is restricted until an agreement exists, and profile guidance prohibits publishing surname/contact details. [Mable discovery](https://mable.com.au/find-support/), [Mable identity privacy](https://mable.com.au/newsroom/most-frequently-asked-questions-by-mable-clients/), [Mable profile guidance](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/create-winning-profile/)
- **Mable ranking is explained:** location, response speed, preferred hours, schedule capacity and feedback affect ordering. Average response speed appears on profiles; its separate response-rate percentage is private to the worker. [Mable ranking](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-support-workers-are-ranked-in-mable-search-results/), [Mable response metrics](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-are-response-rates-calculated-and-why-is-it-important-for-my-business/)
- **Hireup supports controlled invitations:** a client can send one booking request to up to ten workers; the first acceptance confirms the booking. Clients can see when workers already have another booking. This is stronger evidence for team-based discovery than for an unrestricted talent board. [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/13502083241103-Booking-Requests-For-Clients)
- **Sidekicker:** businesses choose screened workers with profiles/reviews, reuse favourites through talent pools, and can block unsuitable workers or use algorithmic selection. Public documentation also describes a reliability score on profiles. [Sidekicker talent tools](https://pages.sidekicker.com/book-a-demo), [Sidekicker profiles](https://sidekicker.com/labour-hire)
- **Airtasker separates quality from reliability:** star ratings/reviews assess experience; completion rate reflects recent fulfilment and appears only after sufficient task history. AU guidance uses the last 20 assigned tasks for Taskers and a five-task display threshold. [Airtasker](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/225875007-What-are-Completion-Rates)
- **ShiftKey facilities can review credentials, invite professionals and maintain favourites.** Its reliability score uses recent 12-month behaviour, including cancellations, missed shifts and arrival time, refreshed after events and nightly. Separate two-way ratings become visible only after sufficient facility feedback. [ShiftKey discovery](https://www.shiftkey.com/facilities/facility-quick-start-guide), [ShiftKey reliability](https://help.shiftkey.com/s/article/Reliability-Score-FAQ-for-Facilities), [ShiftKey ratings](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/beyond-filling-shifts-how-to-build-a-network-of-independent-professionals-you-trust)
- **Clipboard:** facilities can invite favourites back and restrict a worker from future shifts. Its privacy policy distinguishes information shared with facilities generally—such as approximate location, general availability and verification badges—from more detailed records shared for relevant facility relationships. This policy does not establish a particular search UI. [Clipboard controls](https://www.clipboard.com/resources/faq), [Clipboard privacy](https://www.clipboard.com/privacy-policy)
- **Nursa:** facility-defined screening lists compare applicants by unit, shift time and licence; favourites and qualifying applicants can be automatically approved when the facility enables Auto-Schedule. Ineligible clinicians can be removed from visibility. [Nursa selection](https://nursa.com/facility/solutions/healthcare-recruitment-software), [Nursa screening visibility](https://nursa.com/facility/capabilities/background-checks)
- **CareRev:** facilities control favourites and Do Not Send lists; reliability is another selection signal. Favourite/DNS decisions belong to facilities. A universal employer talent-search interface and contact masking are **unverified**. [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/blog/getting-the-most-out-of-carerev-tips-tricks-for-healthcare-professionals)
- **IntelyCare:** terms expressly allow sharing name, contact information and credentials with facilities whose shifts a worker accepts. An open worker directory and pre-booking contact disclosure are **unverified**. [IntelyCare](https://www.intelycare.com/terms-of-service/)
- **Incredible Health:** employers receive candidates matched to skills, experience and job preferences. Its privacy policy permits sharing candidate information with matched employers and contact details to facilitate interviews; employer-specific hiding controls are **unverified**. [Incredible Health](https://www.incrediblehealth.com/privacy/)
- **Instawork:** the Professionals area shows previous workers, with favourites/custom rosters, blocked workers and “okay to return” status. Rosters can be organised by location, role or usual shift availability; requests can prioritise selected workers and optionally fall back to others. [Instawork roster](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/5511147-how-can-i-create-and-manage-my-roster), [Instawork requests](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/2062210-selecting-pros-for-your-shift)
- **Wonolo:** hirers add good workers to Preferred lists; those workers receive early access to future jobs. A general searchable profile board, its filters and contact-masking rules are **unverified**. [Wonolo](https://info.wonolo.com/customer-resources/)
- **Upwork is the strongest general search reference:** filters include skills, hourly rate, feedback, availability, language and location. Workers choose public, logged-in-user-only or private visibility; clients can save talent, invite candidates and make offers. [Upwork search](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063528-How-to-search-for-freelancers-on-Upwork), [Upwork visibility](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060298-How-to-control-who-sees-your-Upwork-profile), [Upwork hiring](https://www.upwork.com/resources/how-to-hire-freelancers)
- **Relief Buddy/Staffy use applicant selection:** employers review applicants’ profiles and choose whom to book. Relief Buddy supports blocking future matches; a comprehensive employer-side talent-search filter set is **unverified** for both. [Relief Buddy workflow](https://apps.apple.com/ml/app/relief-buddy/id1252239337), [Relief Buddy blocking](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/what-happens-when-a-temp-cancels-on-me), [Staffy](https://staffy.com/faq)
- **Florence:** favouriting unlocks private direct invitations and instant booking for suitable shifts. **Locate a Locum:** favourites receive shifts immediately. **Patchwork:** staff-bank teams access submitted profiles/documents and approve membership, rather than operating an unrestricted public board. [Florence](https://help.florence.co.uk/en/articles/8200817-how-to-use-favourites-to-rebook-preferred-professionals), [Locate a Locum](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/12982363-how-to-get-the-most-value-from-your-locums), [Patchwork](https://faq.patchwork.health/i-currently-do-not-work-at-this-organisation-but-want-to-book-bank-shifts.-how-do-i-proceed)
- **LocumDeck gives workers explicit audience control:** only linked, authorised practices see the sessions made bookable to them, with credentials, rates and terms available for the booking decision. [LocumDeck](https://www.nasgp.org.uk/faq/how-does-instant-book-work/)
- **Hire-away policies differ:** Nursa advertises no hire-away fees. Upwork generally requires platform payments during the first two years unless a conversion fee is paid. Sidekicker’s AU agreement specifies direct-employment fees and a possible breach fee where advance notice is omitted. Exact competitor fee amounts should be checked against the applicable contract. [Nursa](https://nursa.com/facility/solutions/healthcare-recruitment-software), [Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043723533-What-is-the-Upwork-Conversion-Fee), [Sidekicker](https://sidekicker.com/policy-pages/terms-of-use)

**3. Availability, matching and freshness**

- **Relief Buddy provides the clearest pharmacy pattern:** recurring weekly available days plus specific unavailable dates. Blackouts override recurring days; both shift search and push/email notifications exclude unavailable dates. [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/available-days-to-work)
- **Relief Buddy also combines practical constraints:** profession, skills, dispensing-software knowledge, minimum hourly rate and maximum distance. Advanced search can deliberately override distance or minimum-rate preferences to widen results. [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/how-do-i-find-a-shift)
- **Locumate:** profiles collect availability, minimum/maximum rates and travel preferences; matching notifications consider availability, rate, software and services. Documentation recommends regularly updating rates/availability; automatic expiry or refresh cadence is **unverified**. [Locumate matching](https://lp-pharmacy.locumate.com.au/pharmacy1), [Locumate maintenance guidance](https://support.locumate.ai/portal/en/kb/locumate/marketplace/locums)
- **LocumCo:** workers update availability in its app, and job alerts can be tailored by location, availability and interests. Recurring patterns, blackout precedence and stale-calendar suppression are **unverified**. [LocumCo](https://locumco.com.au/locumco-candidate/)
- **Mable:** preferred weekdays/hours and work locations influence discovery and matching; workers can hide themselves by selecting “Not available for work.” Its AI assistant can ask suitable workers whether they want shortlisting; a match does not automatically assign work. [Mable availability](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-support-workers-are-ranked-in-mable-search-results/), [Mable pause control](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-are-response-rates-calculated-and-why-is-it-important-for-my-business/), [Mable matching consent](https://mable.com.au/newsroom/ways-to-find-more-work-on-mable/)
- **Mable short-notice work:** Last Minute jobs cover support starting within 4–48 hours and are shown to local workers who indicated availability. This is an urgent matching flow; a continuously live “on-call now” presence indicator is **unverified**. [Mable](https://mable.com.au/newsroom/most-frequently-asked-questions-by-mable-clients/)
- **Hireup:** booking requests expose existing booking conflicts and let workers accept/decline proposed work. Full recurring availability publishing and external-calendar conflict detection are **unverified**. [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/13502083241103-Booking-Requests-For-Clients)
- **Sidekicker:** worker feeds have category, availability and location filters; approved skills/certificates determine additional visible jobs. Public evidence for an employer-visible recurring calendar or freshness timeout is **unverified**. [Sidekicker](https://help.sidekicker.com/en/articles/9035099-how-to-see-more-jobs)
- **Instawork:** workers filter dates, positions and distance and configure schedule/location notifications. Employer outreach cascades through rostered workers, top matching workers and other qualified workers, with timing influenced by urgency, geography and availability. A notification is not a reserved shift. [Instawork worker controls](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/12995914-how-do-i-search-for-shifts), [Instawork matching](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/2062210-selecting-pros-for-your-shift)
- **ShiftKey/Nursa automate approval, not worker consent:** ShiftKey can auto-accept qualifying bids; Nursa can auto-approve requests from favourites or workers meeting configured criteria. A worker still initiates the bid/request. ShiftKey also automatically broadcasts cancellations to configured replacement groups. [ShiftKey approval](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey), [Nursa approval](https://nursa.com/facility/solutions/healthcare-recruitment-software), [ShiftKey recovery](https://help.shiftkey.com/s/article/Cancellation-Recovery---FAQ-for-Facilities)
- **Clipboard/CareRev/IntelyCare:** workers choose open shifts fitting their schedules; Clipboard supports instant booking, CareRev checks shift-specific eligibility, and IntelyCare describes self-selected schedules. A published recurring worker-availability calendar and refresh policy are **unverified** for these products. [Clipboard](https://www.clipboard.com/for-workers), [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/professionals/faq), [IntelyCare](https://www.intelycare.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/IP-Handbook-w-page-version-to-publish43-1.pdf)
- **Staffy/Wonolo:** workers manually choose suitable available shifts/jobs. Staffy documents location/distance and role eligibility; neither cited FAQ establishes recurring worker calendars, blackout handling or automatic availability expiry. Those details remain **unverified**. [Staffy](https://staffy.com/faq), [Wonolo](https://info.wonolo.com/find-work/faqs)
- **Upwork distinguishes capacity from promotion:** profiles show weekly available hours; a separately paid “Available now”/“Open for work” badge is filterable in search. Inactive profiles can automatically become private. That is search hygiene, not evidence of a current, conflict-free shift calendar. [Upwork hours](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063008-How-to-display-your-available-hours-on-Upwork), [Upwork badge](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4403673074707-How-to-use-the-Availability-Badge), [Upwork inactivity](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/115003975967-Why-was-my-profile-changed-to-private)
- **Locate a Locum:** calendar events include Unavailable, Working Elsewhere and Holiday, with date ranges. These personalise search so unavailable dates are excluded; separate filters include minimum rate, company, weekday and emergency shifts. Instant Book access is employer-controlled. [Locate a Locum diary](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/4815841-how-to-use-your-locum-diary), [Locate a Locum search](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/4967810-how-to-find-apply-for-a-shift-as-a-locum)
- **Florence:** Flex workers filter shifts by date, time and distance, then apply; favourites can instantly book eligible shifts. Its separate Rota product handles leave requests and recurring rota templates—these should not be mistaken for verified Flex availability-publishing features. [Florence Flex](https://help.florence.co.uk/en/articles/3362603-how-do-i-book-shifts), [Florence instant booking](https://help.florence.co.uk/en/articles/8200817-how-to-use-favourites-to-rebook-preferred-professionals), [Florence Rota](https://help.florence.co.uk/en/articles/12441559-rota-worker-leave-requests)
- **Patchwork/LocumDeck:** Patchwork offers approved bank workers calendar/filter-based shift selection. LocumDeck goes further: workers publish bookable sessions, authorise specific practices, and define practice-specific work, times and rates; practices can book those sessions directly. [Patchwork](https://patchwork.health/case-studies/staff-bank-network), [LocumDeck](https://www.nasgp.org.uk/faq/how-does-instant-book-work/)

## Top 15 recommendations for ChemistTasker

These are recommended adaptations; proposed timings and controls are not asserted competitor features.

1. **Create separate onboarding tracks for pharmacists, interns, technicians, assistants and Explorers.** Ask only relevant questions and restrict role eligibility explicitly; keep Explorer opportunities clearly labelled. Why: avoids irrelevant licence gates and misleading readiness. Examples: [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-sign-up-as-a-job-seeker-shift-work-inc-ft-pt), [Locate a Locum](https://locatealocum.com/document-checklist).
2. **Separate “can browse,” “can apply,” “can confirm/work” and “payment ready.”** Allow useful previews early, then enforce documented readiness at the appropriate action. Why: preserves discovery while making outstanding obligations clear. Examples: [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/professionals/faq), [Airtasker](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/42073609183641-Having-problems-verifying-my-account-what-should-I-do).
3. **Build a reusable credential passport with verification status, expiry and renewal reminders.** Verify relevant registration, identity and professional cover; attach extra checks to the roles/sites requiring them. Why: reduces repeated uploads and expired-document bookings. Examples: [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey), [Patchwork](https://patchwork.health/products/collaborative-bank).
4. **Keep KYC, work-rights, tax and banking data private and model-specific.** Collect contractor business details or employment/payroll details according to the actual engagement; test payout readiness before first work. Why: prevents unnecessary collection and payment surprises. Examples: [Hireup](https://hireup.com.au/become-a-support-worker/), [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/3300620-w-2-faq-how-to-complete-paperwork-and-start-booking-us-based-pros).
5. **Show an actionable readiness checklist and review status, separately from optional profile completeness.** Explain missing/rejected documents and measure signup → approved → first confirmed shift. Why: workers need a next step, while conversion metrics expose friction. Examples: [Florence](https://www.florence.co.uk/looking-for-work/registration), [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360016020691-What-do-I-need-to-register-as-a-Hireup-support-worker).
6. **Verify pharmacy accounts and authorised contacts before worker outreach; require payment readiness before confirmation.** Why: discovery needs trustworthy hirers and collectible bookings. Examples: [Locumate pharmacy review](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-create-a-pharmacy-account-on-locumate), [Sidekicker payment gate](https://help.sidekicker.com/en/articles/8320035-how-to-add-a-credit-card).
7. **Make pharmacy/site briefs operationally useful.** Capture software, script volume, services and contacts; extend this with staffing, sole-charge expectations, parking, breaks and arrival instructions. Why: workers can assess fit and arrive prepared. Documented comparator fields: [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-create-a-pharmacy-account-on-locumate); the extensions are proposed.
8. **Make Talent Board participation opt-in and audience-controlled.** Default to approved pharmacy accounts; show approximate location and professional information while protecting address, contact details and sensitive documents. Why: supports discovery without uncontrolled exposure. Examples: [Upwork visibility](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060298-How-to-control-who-sees-your-Upwork-profile), [Mable privacy](https://mable.com.au/newsroom/most-frequently-asked-questions-by-mable-clients/).
9. **Launch structured filters for role, distance, exact dates/times, rate, software and service capabilities.** Distinguish essential eligibility from preferences and explain empty results. Why: pharmacy-specific fit matters more than keyword matching. Examples: [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/how-do-i-find-a-shift), [Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063528-How-to-search-for-freelancers-on-Upwork).
10. **Provide preferred lists, private shift invitations and workplace blocking.** Make invitations visibly different from confirmed bookings, and keep blocking distinct from public reviews. Why: repeat relationships improve continuity without confusing booking status. Examples: [Florence](https://help.florence.co.uk/en/articles/8200817-how-to-use-favourites-to-rebook-preferred-professionals), [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/5511147-how-can-i-create-and-manage-my-roster).
11. **Separate reviews, attendance reliability and responsiveness.** Show sample sizes, explain calculations and allow corrections; give new workers a neutral “new” state. Why: one composite star score obscures different risks and disadvantages newcomers. Examples: [ShiftKey](https://help.shiftkey.com/s/article/Reliability-Score-FAQ-for-Facilities), [Airtasker](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/225875007-What-are-Completion-Rates), [Mable](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-are-response-rates-calculated-and-why-is-it-important-for-my-business/).
12. **Use recurring weekly availability plus dated exceptions and confirmed-booking conflicts.** Apply exceptions consistently to search, invitations and notifications. Why: a weekly pattern alone misses holidays and other work. Examples: [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/available-days-to-work), [Locate a Locum](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/4815841-how-to-use-your-locum-diary), [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/13502083241103-Booking-Requests-For-Clients).
13. **Show “availability last confirmed” and offer one-tap reconfirmation/pause.** Proposed launch default: weekly reminders and a visible stale status after 14 days; validate that cadence with users. Why: published availability needs a freshness signal. Related practices: [Locumate update guidance](https://support.locumate.ai/portal/en/kb/locumate/marketplace/locums), [Mable pause control](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/how-are-response-rates-calculated-and-why-is-it-important-for-my-business/).
14. **Match in stages and preserve worker consent.** Offer shifts to eligible available preferred workers first, then widen outreach; add a time-limited urgent-work opt-in. Reserve automatic confirmation for expressly authorised relationships. Why: reduces irrelevant alerts and speeds trusted rebooking. Examples: [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/2062210-selecting-pros-for-your-shift), [Nursa](https://nursa.com/facility/solutions/healthcare-recruitment-software), [LocumDeck](https://www.nasgp.org.uk/faq/how-does-instant-book-work/).
15. **Publish direct-hiring and off-platform payment rules before launch.** Prefer a simple, explicit policy; consider allowing permanent hiring without a hire-away fee while retaining platform safeguards for booked shifts. Why: pharmacies and workers need predictable terms. Contrasting examples: [Nursa’s no-fee approach](https://nursa.com/facility/solutions/healthcare-recruitment-software), [Upwork’s conversion approach](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043723533-What-is-the-Upwork-Conversion-Fee).
