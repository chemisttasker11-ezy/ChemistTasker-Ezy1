## Findings by topic 1-5

### 1. Posting

- **Pharmacy-specific detail:** Locumate’s posting flow collects dates, hours, weekday/weekend rates, travel arrangements, accommodation/flights, solo working, technician numbers, opening/closing duties, immunisation requirements, parking and notes. Base rates exclude super, which is calculated separately. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-post-a-shift)
- **Multi-day coverage:** Locumate explicitly asks whether one professional must cover the whole block or different workers can take individual dates—an important distinction beyond simply copying shifts. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-post-a-shift)
- **Audience targeting:** Locumate documents internal pools, preferred locums and agency distribution, plus immediate or scheduled marketplace release. Automatic timed progression through ChemistTasker’s exact five audiences is **unverified**. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/locumate-december-2024-release-notes)
- **Advanced cascading:** CareRev’s rules engine controls which teams see shifts and when, cascades openings across departments/facilities, and applies maximum-hours, overtime and spending limits. Its model prioritises internal staffing before external labour. [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/carerev-irp)
- **Suggested versus dynamic rates:** ShiftKey offers a suggested rate but lets professionals bid differently. CareRev recommends or automatically adjusts rates using demand and historical performance, within facility-defined minimum/maximum rates. [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey), [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/smart-rates-healthcare-staffing)
- **Requirements and bulk posting:** Relief Buddy collects worker type, specialisations, software knowledge, offered rate and comments. Required skills/software filter out nonmatching workers. Multiple dates can share details while retaining different start/end times. [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/how-do-i-post-a-shift)
- **Templates and duplication:** Instawork’s “book again” copies location, instructions, positions and attire requirements, with dates reviewed before publishing. LocumCo+ advertises editing, duplication and invitations to selected talent. [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/10081823-exploring-the-shift-details-page), [LocumCo+](https://locumco.com.au/latest-news/locumco-plus-manage-locum-shifts/)
- **Private and urgent shifts:** Qwick supports unpublished offers to individuals/groups. It may boost shifts posted within 24 hours or reopened after late cancellation, displaying the additional money as hourly and total values. Private targeting does not establish anonymous employer posting; that remains **unverified**. [Qwick targeting](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5973889), [Qwick boosts](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5564417)
- **Job boards differ from booking systems:** PSA currently directs users to its Locumate partnership, rather than operating separate shift acceptance/payment rules. Hireup’s job board supports one-off, weekly and fortnightly needs. [PSA](https://www.psa.org.au/about/careers/), [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360015006131-The-Job-Board)

### 2. Applying/booking

- **Instant versus reviewed booking:** Locumate offers first-come instant booking and an application path for workers outside a pharmacy’s chosen radius, allowing travel discussions. It advises against the application path for urgent shifts. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-post-a-shift)
- **Configurable auto-accept:** ShiftKey can instantly accept eligible bids using facility preferences, including favourites and reliability-score thresholds of 90+, 80+ or 70+. Other bids remain subject to selection. [ShiftKey](https://marketing.production.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/admin-assist-the-convenience-you-want-with-the-control-you-need)
- **Double-booking prevention:** ShiftKey automatically cancels conflicting bids once one is accepted. Relief Buddy similarly withdraws overlapping applications while preserving nonconflicting applications. This covers bookings inside their platforms, not undisclosed external work. [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey), [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/can-i-apply-to-multiple-shifts-at-the-same-time)
- **Negotiation becomes commitment:** Locate a Locum permits employer-enabled rate negotiation, counter-offers and final offers. Accepting a worker’s proposed rate automatically books them; accepting an employer counter-offer also books the shift. Withdrawal before acceptance differs from cancellation afterward. [Locate a Locum](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/4799126-how-to-negotiate-a-shift-rate-in-the-app)
- **Negotiating time as well as money:** Relief Buddy lets workers request changed hours and, where enabled, rates. Employers compare original/requested terms; acceptance assigns the worker and updates the confirmation. Travel/accommodation allowance changes are excluded from this feature. [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/request-change-request-and-apply-adjusting-shift-rate-or-times)
- **Credential gating:** CareRev requires relevant credentials and facility orientation before claiming shifts. Locate a Locum blocks booking when mandatory credentials expire without an approved replacement, while allowing renewals to be validated alongside still-current documents. [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/professionals/faq), [Locate a Locum](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/15014361-how-to-renew-and-update-my-credentials-on-my-locate-a-locum-app)
- **An offer may not reserve a place:** Qwick can send offers to several applicants; the first professional to confirm receives the shift. Direct offers still require worker confirmation. A universal offer-expiry duration is **unverified**. [Qwick](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5561345), [Qwick direct offers](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5564161)
- **Applicant visibility:** ShiftPosts advertises viewing matching candidates and profiles/ratings before requesting a booking. A fixed applicant cap, number initially displayed, ranking transparency and standard offer-expiry timer are **unverified**. [ShiftPosts](https://shiftposts.com/)

### 3. Agreements

- **Preview and retain the agreement:** Relief Buddy provides a shift-agreement preview before commitment and emails both parties a PDF after acceptance. Its agreement incorporates conduct and cancellation terms. A handwritten digital signature is **unverified**. [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/what-is-a-shift-agreement)
- **Explicit click acceptance:** Locate a Locum requires acceptance of platform terms and supports company SOP acknowledgements and shift-specific requirements before applying, negotiating or instantly booking. Public documentation does not establish the complete timestamp/IP/version audit schema. [Locate a Locum terms](https://locatealocum.com/content/terms/), [Locate a Locum requirements](https://workforce.locatealocum.com/unlimited-locums)
- **Pharmacy operating expectations:** LocumCo sends formal confirmation containing pharmacy details, contract terms and shift information. Pharmacy SOS additionally specifies software competence, pre-shift keys/codes/handover contact and professional duties. [LocumCo](https://locumco.com.au/candidates/locums/), [Pharmacy SOS](https://pharmacysos.com.au/terms-and-conditions/)
- **Breaks and expenses need explicit treatment:** Qwick documents unpaid breaks deducted from paid hours; parking is the worker’s responsibility unless the posting promises paid/validated parking. ShiftPosts’ developer release notes document travel/lodging allowances and worker counter-offers on reimbursements. [Qwick breaks](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5563649), [Qwick parking](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5564673), [ShiftPosts](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/shiftposts-pharmacy-shift-app/id1438860003?platform=ipad)
- **Changes after booking:** Locumate tells pharmacies to obtain the locum’s agreement before support changes accepted shift details; inability to meet changed requirements leads to cancellation assistance. Mable supports sending updated agreements to the client. [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/pharmacy-procedure-for-amending-shift-details-after-locum-accepts-shift), [Mable](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/agreements-how-it-works/)
- **Classification varies materially:** Nursa’s terms describe independent contracting; Hireup directly employs workers; CareRev’s current offering advertises external W-2 clinicians. These models have different payroll, insurance and agreement responsibilities. [Nursa](https://app.nursa.com/settings/terms/terms-of-service), [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360015743411-Information-about-Salary-Pay-and-Taxes), [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/)
- **Electronic signatures are sometimes document-specific:** Nursa requires clinician e-signature on California CDPH 530 forms for payment, with facility signing/download access. Locum Organiser instead attaches the locum’s own terms to emailed booking confirmations; this alone does not prove counterparty signature. [Nursa](https://community.nursa.com/help-center/clinician/own-your-shift/cdph-530/), [Locum Organiser](https://locumorganiser.com/help/booking-confirmations/)

### 4. Cancellation/no-show

- **Locumate:** Pricing guidance says pharmacy cancellation within 72 hours requires 50% of the locum’s shift fee plus the service fee; beyond 72 hours no cancellation fee applies. Locums self-cancel beyond 30 days but contact support inside 30 days. Published engagement terms also mention 48-hour termination notice: exact policy precedence is **unverified**. [Pricing](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/locumate-pricing-information), [Worker cancellation](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-do-i-cancel-a-shift), [Terms](https://app.locumate.com.au/terms-and-conditions)
- **Pharmacy SOS:** Employer terms specify 48 hours’ notice or placement fee plus three hours’ worker pay, subject to exceptions/suitable replacement work. Worker terms instead mention 24 hours: the operative threshold is **unverified**. Agency cancellation/no-show can trigger placement-fee refunds or waivers, including future-invoice credit options. [Pharmacy SOS](https://pharmacysos.com.au/terms-and-conditions/)
- **Relief Buddy:** Provider cancellation is free at least 14 days ahead; between 72 hours and 14 days costs C$100; under 72 hours costs the full shift fees. Worker equivalents are free/C$100/C$200. Partner-network terms may differ; repeated cancellation can lead to banning. Exact boundary treatment at 72 hours is **unverified**. [Relief Buddy](https://reliefbuddy.com/terms-of-service/)
- **Hireup:** Client cancellation with over 12 hours’ notice is free; 2–12 hours incurs two hours; under two hours incurs the full shift. It encourages mutually agreed rescheduling first and records late cancellation through the booking/payment process. [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360031493231-Statement-on-Cancellations)
- **Mable:** Ordinary cancellation terms are negotiated in the service agreement. Its separate Last Minute policy requires full payment for client cancellation within two hours; two consecutive cancellations by either side may remove Last Minute access. [Mable agreements](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/agreements-how-it-works/), [Mable Last Minute](https://mable.com.au/cancellation-policy/)
- **Nursa worker ladder:** Fifth early cancellation or second late cancellation in 30 days triggers a 14-day request freeze; five late cancellations in 60 days triggers banning. First/second no-shows trigger 14-day restrictions; a third lifetime no-show triggers banning. No-show means not working without cancelling in-app before start; a minute-based lateness threshold is **unverified**. [Nursa](https://community.nursa.com/help-center/clinician/own-your-shift/cancelation-ncns/)
- **Qwick worker ladder and appeals:** Its September 2026 policy lists permanent suspension for first-shift no-show/unexcused call-out; otherwise five days, fourteen days, then permanent suspension. Temporary suspensions preserve existing bookings. It allows fifteen minutes for accidental acceptance cancellation and considers documented emergencies individually. [Qwick](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5560641)
- **Instawork lateness/no-show:** Fifteen-minute lateness is a major tardy. Its general cancellation policy defines automatic no-show at 60 minutes, but separate lateness guidance allows a business to mark no-show/refuse work earlier when the worker fails to communicate/check in. Sixty minutes is therefore not a guaranteed arrival grace period. [Reliability](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/5048510-how-reliability-affects-your-shift-access), [Cancellation](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/6122001-cancellation-policy), [Lateness](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/3614637-what-happens-if-i-m-late-to-my-shift)
- **Facility compensation:** Instawork generally charges up to four hours for cancellation inside 24 hours, with contract-specific exceptions. Qwick likewise specifies up to four hours plus applicable fees. Wonolo generally charges 50% inside twelve hours, including substantial last-minute changes. [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/2062211-what-happens-if-i-need-to-cancel-my-scheduled-professional-s), [Qwick](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5567169), [Wonolo](https://support-business.wonolo.com/en_us/what-is-the-cancellation-policy-for-requestors-rJFSm4jTxg)
- **Replacement automation:** ShiftKey reopens cancelled shifts immediately; optional Cancellation Recovery offers late-cancelled shifts to favourites, reliable professionals or a wider pool according to facility settings. Inside two hours, professionals must call the facility. [ShiftKey](https://help.shiftkey.com/s/article/Cancellation-Recovery---FAQ-for-Facilities)
- **UK policy inconsistency:** Locate a Locum reviews cancellation reasons individually, including illness/emergencies. Its current public terms permit discretionary £25 fees for either party cancelling inside 28 days and £50 without notification. Actual enforcement against employer-specific policies is **unverified**. [Help policy](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/3815156-locate-a-locum-cancellation-policy-locums), [Terms](https://locatealocum.com/content/terms/)

### 5. Payment

- **Locumate:** Free registration/posting; A$50 excluding GST per completed marketplace shift, invoiced with seven-day payment terms. Timesheets auto-approve after 48 hours; invoices are distributed Tuesdays and include bank details for pharmacy-to-locum payment. This is different from prepaying a booking fee to confirm. [Pricing](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/locumate-pricing-information), [Payment process](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/payment-process)
- **Relief Buddy:** Published pharmacy base administration fee is 12.5%, normally subject to C$40 minimum/C$65 maximum for eligible accounts; negotiated terms vary. Billing follows approved hours; the worker submits first, and owner inaction auto-approves after three business days. Completed-shift fees are generally nonrefundable. [Terms](https://reliefbuddy.com/terms-of-service/), [Hours approval](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/how-do-i-confirm-shift-hours-once-a-shift-is-complete)
- **Nursa:** Eligible shifts can receive instant Nursa Direct payment. Traditional deposits generally arrive within one–three business days after processing; facilities have two business days to confirm reports before automatic verification. A universal published platform percentage is **unverified**. [Nursa](https://community.nursa.com/help-center/clinician/get-paid/payments/when-do-i-get-paid/)
- **CareRev and ShiftKey:** CareRev publishes Tuesday/Friday deposits; ShiftKey publishes Tuesday direct deposits with approval cutoffs and optional faster methods. Universal facility markup/refund schedules are **unverified**. [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/professionals/faq), [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/using-shiftkey-understanding-your-payment-options)
- **Instawork:** Businesses select an all-inclusive hourly bill rate with market recommendations. US weekly deposits generally arrive Wednesday for W-2 shifts and Friday for contractor shifts. Hours-adjustment disputes auto-approve after 72 hours without a partner response. [Pricing](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/5854747-instawork-business-pricing), [Payouts](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/4116128-earnings-payment-schedules), [Disputes](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/13113958-how-does-instawork-handle-payment-processing-when-there-are-disputes-about-worked-hours)
- **Qwick:** Normal onsite clock activity can auto-approve immediately; flagged shifts have a 48-hour review window. Contractor Instant Pay costs 4%, potentially arriving around thirty minutes after verification; standard transfers have no payout fee but take longer. W-2 payroll is separate. [Verification](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/13038785), [Contractor payouts](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5565313), [W-2 payroll](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5643329)
- **Mable and Hireup:** Mable adds a 7.95% client fee and deducts a 10% worker fee; eligible opted-in invoices can be paid within ten days of approval. Hireup uses published employment-based rates, fortnightly worker payroll and weekly client invoices. [Mable pricing](https://mable.com.au/pricing/), [Faster payments](https://mable.com.au/legal/terms/faster-payments/), [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360038670392-Information-About-Funding-Types-and-Payment-Options)
- **UK fee choices:** Locate a Locum’s terms specify £20 plus VAT marketplace fees, payable following completed sessions, alongside negotiated subscription/direct-locum arrangements. Locum Organiser charges £15.99 monthly for its administration tools; it is not the worker’s payroll provider. [Locate a Locum](https://locatealocum.com/content/terms/), [Locum Organiser](https://locumorganiser.com/)
- **ShiftPosts limitations:** Workers retain their stated earnings; published pricing identifies additional processing fees of 2.9% plus $0.30. Current base platform charges, cancellation/refund rules and guaranteed payout deadlines are **unverified**; older promotional payout claims should not be treated as contractual guarantees. [ShiftPosts pricing](https://shiftposts.com/pricing/)

## Top 15 recommendations for ChemistTasker

These are proposed product decisions informed by the evidence, not claims that every comparator implements the complete recommendation.

1. **Make reservation and confirmation unmistakable:** Show “Reserved—pharmacy payment required” to both parties, including deadline and consequences; show “Confirmed” only after successful payment. Why: workers must know when commitment becomes binding. Comparable status separation: [Qwick](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5561345), [Locate a Locum](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/4799126-how-to-negotiate-a-shift-rate-in-the-app).
2. **Collect payment details before external booking; expire unpaid reservations automatically.** Display a countdown, send reminders, release both shift and worker on expiry, and protect against duplicate confirmation. Why: prevent unpaid holds blocking availability. Exact competitor payment-pending equivalent and optimal timeout: **unverified**; Mable gates agreement acceptance on verified payment details. [Mable](https://mable.com.au/help_centre/agreements-how-it-works/)
3. **Keep all five audience stages on one shift record.** Give each stage a release time, manual advance option and urgent bypass; stop further distribution when reserved. Why: preserve trusted staffing without creating competing copies. Closest precedents: [CareRev cascading](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/carerev-irp), [Locumate pools](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/locumate-december-2024-release-notes).
4. **Use a reusable pharmacy profile plus explicit shift overrides.** Include role, software, duties, staffing/solo status, breaks, opening/closing, parking, travel and accommodation. Why: prevent avoidable mismatches and repetitive entry. Platforms: [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-post-a-shift), [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/how-do-i-post-a-shift).
5. **Support independent dates, recurring shifts and indivisible blocks.** Make “same worker required for all dates” explicit. Why: continuity requirements differ from filling individual vacancies. Platforms: [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-post-a-shift), [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360015006131-The-Job-Board).
6. **Launch with fixed rates, optional negotiation and visible total costs.** Separate worker rate, super treatment, GST, expenses and booking fee; introduce suggested rates before automated surge pricing. Why: transparent launch economics with room for negotiated rural/urgent work. Platforms: [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey), [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/solutions/smart-rates-healthcare-staffing).
7. **Offer reviewed applications and opt-in instant booking.** Restrict auto-accept to qualified favourites or clear eligibility rules. Why: urgency and existing trust justify different booking paths. Platforms: [ShiftKey](https://marketing.production.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/admin-assist-the-convenience-you-want-with-the-control-you-need), [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-post-a-shift).
8. **Block conflicts at reservation and confirmation.** Withdraw conflicting applications, recheck availability before payment completes, and preserve nonconflicting applications. Why: eliminate internal double-booking races. Platforms implement automatic conflict withdrawal: [ShiftKey](https://www.shiftkey.com/resources/blog/essential-tips-for-professionals-using-shiftkey), [Relief Buddy](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/can-i-apply-to-multiple-shifts-at-the-same-time).
9. **Gate regulated roles with current, relevant credentials.** Validate registration, insurance and required competencies, including validity on the shift date; provide renewal reminders. Why: a favourite must still be eligible. Platforms: [CareRev](https://www.carerev.com/professionals/faq), [Locate a Locum](https://help.locatealocum.com/en/articles/15014361-how-to-renew-and-update-my-credentials-on-my-locate-a-locum-app).
10. **Issue a versioned booking agreement to both parties.** Capture accepted terms, actor, timestamp and policy version; provide a downloadable copy. Require explicit re-consent for material changes. Why: prevent disputes about changed hours, rates or duties. Platforms: [Relief Buddy PDFs](https://knowledgebase.reliefbuddy.com/what-is-a-shift-agreement), [Locumate amendments](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/pharmacy-procedure-for-amending-shift-details-after-locum-accepts-shift).
11. **Establish Australian engagement and super rules before launch.** Distinguish existing employees, pharmacy-engaged contractors and any employed-worker offering. Why: an ABN or contractor checkbox does not settle classification or super obligations. Comparator models: Hireup employees/Nursa contractors; governing guidance: [Fair Work](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/find-help-for/independent-contractors/whole-of-relationship-test), [ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/super-for-employers/work-out-if-you-have-to-pay-super/super-for-independent-contractors).
12. **Publish one cancellation matrix covering both parties and every booking state.** Specify exact boundaries, worker compensation, platform-fee refunds, mutual cancellation and emergency exceptions. Why: conflicting help/contract windows undermine trust. Useful models: [Hireup](https://help.hireup.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/360031493231-Statement-on-Cancellations), [Relief Buddy](https://reliefbuddy.com/terms-of-service/).
13. **Separate lateness, no-show and accidental booking; provide appeals.** Add an ETA/contact flow and evidence review before serious sanctions; publish warning/restriction/suspension rules. Why: missed app check-in and failure to attend are different events. Platforms: [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/3614637-what-happens-if-i-m-late-to-my-shift), [Qwick](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/5560641).
14. **Automatically seek replacements and make fee remedies explicit.** Reopen cancelled confirmed shifts, notify favourites first, then widen distribution; offer a booking-fee refund or optional replacement credit when delivery fails. Why: pharmacies need coverage and a clear remedy. Platforms: [ShiftKey recovery](https://help.shiftkey.com/s/article/Cancellation-Recovery---FAQ-for-Facilities), [Pharmacy SOS guarantees](https://pharmacysos.com.au/terms-and-conditions/).
15. **Separate booking-fee collection from worker remuneration and timesheet disputes.** State who pays the worker and when; adopt a visible 48-hour approval deadline, automatic approval and a dispute path. Why: a paid booking fee must not imply wages have been paid or guaranteed. Platforms: [Locumate](https://support.locumate.com.au/portal/en/kb/articles/payment-process), [Qwick](https://support.qwick.com/en/articles/13038785), [Instawork](https://help.instawork.com/en/articles/13113958-how-does-instawork-handle-payment-processing-when-there-are-disputes-about-worked-hours).
