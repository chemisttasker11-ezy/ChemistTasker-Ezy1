# Agent instructions — Australian public holidays & MA000012

Always read this file BEFORE modifying `shifts/pricing.py`, `shifts/pricing_data.py`, `public_holidays.json`, `public_holidays_local.json` or the employee payroll/engagement award calculations.

1. **Legal rule:** A public holiday for NES purposes is a public holiday declared for the employee's **base-for-work region**, not automatically the physical job site. Fair Work: https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/public-holidays ; Act section 115. Resolve work base explicitly for cross-region/transferred employees. Do not infer locality from postcode alone.
2. **Penalty entitlement:** Pharmacy Industry Award 2020 MA000012 clauses 22 and 28: public holiday worked ordinary hours are **225% permanent / 250% casual** of applicable minimum hourly rate (casual includes loading); overtime clause 21 has distinct rates; do not stack ordinary penalty and overtime. Award substitution by employee/employer agreement can change the applicable day/part-day. https://awards.fairwork.gov.au/MA000012.html
3. **Classification:** Only legally declared **public holidays** or declared **part-day public holidays** are award public holidays. **NSW Local Event Days and NSW Bank Holiday** are NOT pharmacy public holidays; NSW gazetted Local Public Holidays are. https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/public-holidays and https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/public-holidays/local-public-holidays
4. **Geography:** QLD Brisbane Ekka, Gold Coast Show and Cairns Show differ. Regional NT Show Days, TAS Regatta vs Recreation Day, regional WA King's Birthday replacement and VIC Melbourne Cup regional substitutions must be resolved at council/declared boundary. A holiday for Gold Coast is not one for Brisbane. Some QLD council boundaries cover only a *part* of one LGA; these must use separate exact locality codes, not a blanket council flag.
5. **Time:** QLD Christmas Eve is 18:00-midnight; SA/NT Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve are 19:00-midnight. NSW local public holidays can be part-day at 09:00, noon or 13:00. Split shifts at every boundary and across midnight.
6. **Year and source:** Maintain *both* 2026/2027 statewide dates in `public_holidays.json`, and local/part-day/exclusions with source URLs and explicit `pending_years` in `public_holidays_local.json`. Check the jurisdiction's OFFICIAL calendars; FWO 2027 is an overview, not exhaustive. Do **not** predict undeclared 2027 show dates or the 2027 VIC AFL Friday. Never treat a region without its year verified as certified.
7. **Source and change control:** `shifts.holidays` is the ONLY resolver. `shifts.pricing` must never duplicate council/date rules. The pharmacy's explicitly configured `public_holiday_region` is a work-base *preview* default; an employee's actual base-for-work may differ. Any agreed substituted holiday must be documented in immutable engagement terms and tested; do not silently treat the shift site as the employee's work base.
8. **Status contract:** `REGION_REQUIRED`, `LOCAL_DECLARATION_PENDING`, `YEAR_NOT_CERTIFIED`, `UNKNOWN_STATE` all require review before employee payroll. Date-only `public_holiday_dates` is exclusively full-day; `public_holiday_windows` holds part-days, and `public_holiday_calendar_status` exposes pending years. Clients may not present an uncertain date as legally certified.
9. **Testing gate:** Run `python backend/shifts/data/reconcile_public_holidays.py --check`, then `python backend/manage.py test qa_release.test_pr201_phase6_b14 qa_release.test_pr201_phase6_holidays qa_release.test_pr201_phase6_award_floor shifts.test_pricing_contract`; verify all 8 states x both years, 3+ local boundaries, weekend substitute vs additional dates, part-day and overnight splits, and permanent/casual award tables against Fair Work.
10. **Release rule:** If automatic payroll depends on an unconfirmed region/year, demand explicit verification and do not silently apply the statewide rate as if all local declarations are known. Maintain auditable source URL, effective year, authority and scope. Archive a dated government source/evidence link in docs/qa. No source-grounded holiday may be silently deleted merely to make a fingerprint pass.

This directory's reconciliation script is a **repeatable offline validator**, not a scraper. When official government lists change, update the data, validate, then commit the revised evidence and regression tests. No real payroll calculation is certified solely by passing this script.
